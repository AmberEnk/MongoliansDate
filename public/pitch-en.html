Why this exists

Яагаад?

Even in a big city it is hard to bump into other Mongolians. English Reddit is not built around us. Uchral is a space for our conversations.

What it is

Энэ юу вэ?

  • Anyone can read without an account
  • Sign up to post, reply, and start a community
  • 18+
  • Not in the App Store — open it on your phone and add it to the home screen

What you can do

Юу хийж болох вэ?

  • Home, Popular, News — a Reddit-style feed
  • Explore — city and topic communities
  • Mine — the groups and posts you created
  • Dating — optional listings, not required

Dating is optional

Танилцах нь сонголт

Uchral is not a dating app. You can post a short listing if you want. The heart of it is conversation, questions, and city groups.
